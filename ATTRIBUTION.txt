# Circuit provenance and license

The bundled initial circuit is **FlyWire FAFB v783**, a curated 668-neuron
escape/steering subgraph. It is **not the male-CNS dataset** from Franky's
earlier project. That bulk acquisition is blocked in this execution session.

Retrieved as data only from:
https://github.com/imrizwan/flywire-cortex/blob/a72a0b04d9cdf7383f57a0e0186b9bb1e9c1ae45/data/circuit.seed.json

Curator: imrizwan/flywire-cortex contributors. Their source file describes the
extract as `FlyWire Codex FAFB v783 connections.csv (syn>=5, signed by nt_type)`.
Their ETL was inspected at the same commit; it signs contact counts by predicted
transmitter. Inhibitory glutamate, positive fallback for missing transmitter,
and half-strength monoamines are **model assumptions**, not measured individual
synaptic physiology. The original bulk contacts have not been independently
revalidated in this session. Source SHA-256 and processed counts are recorded in
`evidence/circuit-build.json` and inside the application bundle.

Original research:

- Dorkenwald, S. et al. *Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.* Nature 634,
  124–138 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07558-y
- Schlegel, P. et al. *Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of
  Drosophila.* Nature 634, 139–152 (2024).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5
- FlyWire Consortium: https://flywire.ai/ and https://codex.flywire.ai/

The neuron model, adaptation, light/tilt/loom input assignment, and motor decoder
in Mote are authored mathematical assumptions. No learning is implemented.
Different trajectories do not establish a biological advantage. No complete
brain simulation, animal-behavior equivalence, or consciousness is claimed.
