# Mote Darwin data attribution

Mote Darwin is an independent experimental application, not an official FlyWire product.

## Connectivity

FlyWire Consortium, **FlyWire Whole-brain Connectome Connectivity Data**, release 783.0 (2024).
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10676866
License recorded by the release: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Inputs: `proofread_root_ids_783.npy` and `proofread_connections_783.feather`.
The binary export changes storage order and representation. Every proofread ID,
every connection row and its synapse count is preserved; no extra count threshold
is applied. Neuropil labels and per-row transmitter probability vectors are not
included in the runtime binary. See manifest.json for hashes and exact counts.

## Neuron annotations

Derived sensory/output flags, transmitter classifications and anchor positions
come from flyconnectome/flywire_annotations, pinned commit
`a83b2776d60d5764cef36b927f5f9679c16c47a2`:
https://github.com/flyconnectome/flywire_annotations

The upstream repository requests the following citations for its current data:
- Schlegel et al. (2024), *Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila*.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5
- Matsliah et al. (2024), *Neuronal parts list and wiring diagram for a visual system*.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07981-1
- Berg et al. (2026), *Sexual dimorphism in the complete Drosophila male central nervous system connectome*.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.015
- Tastekin et al. (2026), *The complete gustatory connectome of adult Drosophila reveals how taste guides feeding, foraging, and social behavior*.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2026.08.016
- Dorkenwald et al. (2024), cited above.

The annotation repository has no separate LICENSE file at the pinned revision;
the connectivity release's license is not asserted to cover that repository.
Original annotation tables are fetched for reproducibility and are not republished
in the package. Positions are neuron anchor coordinates, not complete skeletons.

## Authored model

The simulation equations, normalized signed weights, sensory stimulus strengths,
adaptation, visual sampling and creature motor mapping are authored choices.
GABA, glutamate and explicitly annotated histamine are modeled as inhibitory;
other/unknown transmitters are treated as excitatory. Modulatory, receptor,
cell-specific physiological and synapse-time dynamics are not reconstructed.
Darwin optimizes 472 inherited group parameters by genetic selection between generations. It does not implement within-lifetime learning, recover memories, claim consciousness or establish biological validation.
The full proofread graph does not include every unproofread fragment in the
larger raw synapse table. Raw microscopy images are not part of this application.
