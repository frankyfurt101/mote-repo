Mote Village

Four woodland creatures build a village, guided by a little council living on your phone.

  • Fully autonomous foraging, chopping, mining and carrying.
  • Lodge, garden, granary, workshop, well and cottage; ongoing gardening, repairs and toolmaking.
  • Three council advisers share a Qwen3.5-0.8B language model. Generated advice chooses work; urgent needs take priority.
  • A one-time optional 533 MB Wi-Fi download. No gameplay is sent to a cloud model.
  • Four independent brain states sharing the complete 139,255-neuron, 16,847,997-row FlyWire graph.
  • Starts from a copied Darwin champion genome. Existing apps and saves stay separate.
  • Automatic saves, creature inspection, pause and double-speed world time.
  • Read-only USB diagnostics for direct troubleshooting.

Version 0.2.0-1 · rootless iOS 16. Optional council model: 533 MB additional Wi-Fi download, then offline inference.

Game rules implement survival, tools and navigation. Experimental council advice influences individual job assignments; it can be mistaken. Four connectome states modulate movement; Colony does not perform further training or demonstrate biological cognition. Runs while visible; no offline catch-up. Performance on the physical XR is awaiting verification. Existing Colony saves migrate with an untouched legacy copy.
